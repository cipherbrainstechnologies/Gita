# Investment & execution thesis

## Concept
**VERSE//MODE** sells identity and ideas expressed through highly wearable T-shirts, oversized tees, track pants/joggers and gym layers. Two creative worlds: `THOUGHTWEAR` (original aphorisms / properly sourced classical thought / desi wit / existential humor) and `MOTIONWEAR` (training ethos / movement / progress). Customer promise: *wear your state of mind.* Translation and classical-source claims demand specialist review; fun and irony must avoid preachiness.

**Personas:** college 18–24 (primary paying/co-creator group), first-job 25–30, active creative 30–35, 15–17 catalog browsing audience with no personalised tracking/ads. Offer unisex sizing while displaying actual fit on diverse consenting adults; no beauty-altered body types.

## What NOT to do
- Do not publish 1,000 variants just to show depth. A tight 20–30 SKU founding capsule is easier to quality-control and fulfill. 1,000 lines + 1,000 designs form a private **creative backlog**, not retail inventory.
- Do not take preorder payments before vendor and fulfilment windows are contractually validated, and clearly disclose expected ship windows and cancellation rights.
- Do not duplicate competitor wording, imagery or prints. No fake testimonials, fake discounts, fake available stock, fake “hand-picked Sanskrit” attributions.
- No virtual-try-on style recommendation claims without validated size measurements.
- No uncontrolled coupon stacking, indefinite exclusive arrangements or franchise promises on day one.

## Phased business model
1. **Discovery/waitlist** (no paid orders): click-to-vote designs, sample testing, social validation and verified newsletter consent. For minors, avoid personal data until compliant flow approved.
2. **Preorder pilot**: 12–30 approved designs, 2 garment bases, at most 2 core fits, INR-priced; fulfil 30–100 real orders with an identified production partner; avoid large MOQ risk. Clearly advertise the fact that items are made after ordering.
3. **Repeat drop engine**: constrained runs, reorder and return analytics, customer community, category/seasonal/campus/partner coupons, adult birthday offers with explicit DOB consent.
4. **In-store experience**: company-operated kiosk POS; after unit economics, legally reviewed franchise model and settlement.

## Illustrative unit economics (NOT a market quote)
Sample tee ticket ₹999 (tax-inclusive); assumed garment+printing ₹440, packing ₹35, shipping/RTO average ₹100, gateway ₹25, refunds/defects reserve ₹60, marketing CAC ₹150 = ₹189 contribution **before overhead and applicable tax accounting**. Track pants might have very different margins; obtain multiple supplier quotes and validate GST on actual garments. The product requires a live per-SKU margin calculator; do not present example as guaranteed profit.

## Operating cadence
Daily: preorder aging, refunds, service SLAs, production batches, defects, paid media spend and cash reconciliation. Weekly: gross margin by drop/size, conversion, abandoned checkout, payment failures, reorder rate, supplier on-time, NPS, return size reasons, novel design yield. Monthly: debt-free inventory exposure, payout reconciliation, chargebacks, tax close, cohort repeat purchase, design-vs-sellthrough review.
