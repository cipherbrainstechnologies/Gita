# Franchise and POS — deliberately deferred from first cash launch

**Legal model decision:** Initially the legal D2C operating entity sells all online preorders and is responsible for invoicing/returns. At a company-operated pop-up/POS, ownership of physical stock remains with this seller. A future franchise must explicitly identify merchant-of-record for walk-in purchase, inventory owner, invoice issuer, commission/payout and consumer-redressal parties; never silently switch ownership in the app.

**Flow:** HQ creates product/SKU -> approved physical stock received into warehouse -> transfer order -> accept scanned units at store -> cashier scans SKU (physical-only unless acknowledged ship-to-store) -> tax calculations -> provider tender or cash -> immutable receipt -> daily cash closing -> return/refund and credit-note -> audit & settlement.

**Store roles:** HQ finance/operations, franchise principal, store manager, cashier, stock clerk, support; least privilege enforced for store scope. Franchise principal cannot access all customer data. Stock adjustments demand reason and optionally second approver; all movements ledger-based.

**Franchise feasibility milestones:** one company store, measured footfall and fit conversion; supplier SLA, shipping economics, shrinkage, receipt tax treatment, agreements, field training, POS offline conflict handling, cash controls, brand standards and royalty basis. Only then switch on partner onboarding and commissions.

**Pilot success metrics:** per-store gross profit, return reason by fit, SKU accuracy, batch discrepancy, customer satisfaction, cashier reconciliation and repeat visits. Avoid promising potential partners guaranteed financial returns.
