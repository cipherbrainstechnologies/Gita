# Finance operating model — original assumptions not live supplier quotes

**For each SKU**: `net selling revenue (ex tax) – landed apparel+printing – packing – gateway fees – outward shipping – expected return/reverse logistics – defects reserve – allocated creator/CAC = unit contribution`. Do not use sales price gross of collected GST as revenue. Compare product variants by size and fulfillment zone.

| Item | Example tee | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Customer displayed price | ₹999 | illustrative inclusive of tax |
| Apparel blank + printing | ₹440 | estimate, get written quote |
| Packaging | ₹35 | branded mailer and care card |
| Payment processing | ₹25 | provider schedule/GST to verify |
| Forward + expected reverse/RTO | ₹100 | varies with PIN |
| Defects/returns reserve | ₹60 | estimate, adjust from actual |
| Acquisition cost | ₹150 | test real campaigns |
| Pre-tax/proper tax-accounting illustrative remainder | ₹189 | *not net profit* |

**Scenarios, not forecasts:** 50 orders × ₹189 = ₹9,450 pre-overhead contribution; 100 × ₹189 = ₹18,900; 500 × ₹189 = ₹94,500, before tax-effect, salaries, platform, samples, refunds above reserve, fixed overhead and losses. Do not confuse gross merchandise value with revenue or profit.

**Cash-risk guardrails:** cap each run of physical samples; require supplier deposit/production terms; hold payment refunds reserve; never use all customer preorder cash on marketing. Map gateway settlement cycle vs vendor milestones, refund timing, chargeback risk and GST liabilities weekly. Restrict automatic discount to positive contribution margin and pricing consent.

**Financial experiments:** evaluate tees at ₹799/₹999/₹1,199 and pants at ₹1,299/₹1,499/₹1,699 only after real COGS. Discount claims rely on recorded pricing history and reference price law, not fabricated MRP.
