## Preorder policy v0
An item marked PREORDER is not in physical ready-to-ship stock. Each listing displays the currently confirmed estimated dispatch **range**, not a shipping guarantee; payment and production are tracked separately. Before purchase, the customer sees the total amount and expected dispatch schedule. We notify customers when confirmed production dates materially change. We provide a clear cancellation request route. If we cannot manufacture/supply within the agreed timeframe, applicable law and the agreed sale terms govern cancellation and refunds. Customization limits do not remove statutory rights for defective, misdescribed or wrong goods.

## Return, refund and exchange v0
Customers may request help using `[SUPPORT_LINK]` and quote their order number. The policy's ordinary change-of-mind period, proof requirements, return freight and sizing exchange terms must be completed and prominently displayed before sales. Statutory remedies for defective, damaged, incorrect or materially misdescribed products apply regardless of discretionary exclusions. Approved refunds go to the original payment method where feasible, with gateway and banking processing estimates displayed transparently. No blanket 'non-refundable' label intended to override law.

## Shipping v0
We ship to eligible Indian PIN codes confirmed by our carrier integration. Preorder production time and courier transit time are distinct; each product shows a manufacturing dispatch window, and the shipping quote separately shows a carrier estimate. Courier delays and failed delivery attempts are tracked and communicated. Customers receive AWB/tracking only after a shipment is actually booked.

## Privacy & communication v0
We use necessary information to fulfill sales, authenticate accounts, respond to support and comply with legal recordkeeping. Optional marketing channels need separate purpose choices, transparent unsubscribe and retention controls. If operating for a 15–17 audience, we do not run behavioural targeted advertising to minors and we require approved parental-consent mechanisms before opening personal accounts to children if required by law. Third-party processing, international transfer, grievance/privacy contact, retention periods and access/deletion/export processes are to be specified by counsel.

## Offers & birthday v0
Promotions have named start/end dates (IST), eligible SKUs, stack rules, maximum redemption count, discount cap and exclusions shown before checkout. Birthday rewards apply only to verified eligible adult accounts with voluntary birthday data and the applicable offer terms. A partnership discount must disclose affiliate/employee eligibility and abuse prevention; no hidden charges. Struck-through prices must reflect lawful comparison prices.

Brand/IP permission, term/territory, training, POS system fees, ownership and title to inventory, manufacturing and transfer pricing, cash/card merchant-of-record, tax point, store location/data governance, commission/payment cycle, returns, service SLAs, audit/termination and customer grievance allocation.
