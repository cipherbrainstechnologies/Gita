# India legal/compliance checklist — external lawyer/CA signoff required

**Do not clone The Souled Store legal terms.** This project includes original drafting scaffolds; inserting somebody else's business name does not give enforceable or accurate protections. Legal entity details, policies, cancellation windows, tax, shipping promised dates and customer-care details must be real and consistent with platform behavior. Legal drafts are **not approved for live display** until signed off.

- Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 + 2026 amendment **effective 1 January 2027**: seller/brand entity, support/grievance details, truthful price reductions including prior price rules, no dark patterns; relevant product origin/importer, return/refund/delivery information. Validate exact applicability for direct inventory model versus marketplace.
- GST: classification according to actual apparel HSN, ₹2,500 per-piece threshold and prevailing rates; 5% below threshold in referenced 2025 government release, higher threshold commonly 18%; accountant verifies before invoices and assesses registration/e-invoice rules.
- Legal Metrology Packaged Commodities: product/label declarations, MRP, country of origin, manufacturer/packer, size, customer-care, and any exceptions. Confirm with compliance adviser.
- DPDP Act 2023 / Rules 2025 phased commencement: personal data principles, consent, purpose, retention, security, children's verifiable parental consent where applicable and bar on targeted ads/tracking directed at children. Set purchase accounts 18+ before counsel signs off alternative.
- SMS DLT/TCCCPR, WhatsApp business templates, email spam/consent laws. Separate transactional messaging from promotional opt-in, easy stop action and data minimization.
- Intellectual property: do not reuse Souled Store art, quotes/protected graphics or third-party model photos, music, fonts or typefaces without rights. Ancient quote provenance is a scholarly+copyright matter; modern translation may be copyrighted.
- Accessibility, promotional claims, influencer/affiliate disclosures, franchise legal/tax and labour/transfer documents.

## Policy approval workflow
DRAFT -> COUNSEL_REVIEW -> FINANCE_REVIEW -> FOUNDER_APPROVED -> EFFECTIVE. Store version and digest for every accepted policy with order snapshots. Never silently alter accepted terms retrospectively.
