# Brand system, editorial rules and demand validation

## Voice
Bold, introspective, clever, not mean. More than gym motivation; self-aware humor, friends who challenge us, discipline, quiet, college deadlines, social battery, ambition, friendship, identity. Example ORIGINAL English candidates:
- `Real friends question the script.`
- `Quiet is a power move.`
- `Less proving. More becoming.`
- `A good argument is still care.`
- `Train the habit, not the highlight.`
- `Not every notification deserves you.`

These are newly authored concepts, **not translations** and not claimed ancient Sanskrit verses. The user's sample suggests the theme of honest disagreement in friendship; design a fresh expression rather than trace or reproduce someone else's composition.

## Collections
FRENEMY? (friendship that challenges), STATIC / NOISE (attention economy), AFTER CLASS (campus wit), UNFINISHED (growth), RESET (gym), ORIGIN (properly researched classical thought), NIGHT SHIFT (creators), QUIET REBELS.

## Acquisition
Micro-creator partnerships with 18+ students and early-career creators; paid UGC rights and FTC/ASCI advertising disclosure. Original short-form videos, campus pop-ups and voting before manufacturing. Don't incentivize fake ratings, run manipulative urgency or market personalized ads to children.

## 90-day validation experiments
Site waitlist + polls (legally compliant consent), 3 design directions, size/fit tests, real printed samples, paid preorders on small limited collections. A/B test humor vs philosophy vs gym art; measure willingness-to-pay, retention and refund rate. Partner codes have clear terms and non-self-referral rules. Birthday offers require voluntary DOB and eligibility validation for adults.
